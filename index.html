ST/01 · About

Astro Synapse is an independent lab that researches, builds, and ships AI systems for companies that need them to hold up in production.

We design for longevity: data first, trust always, built on infrastructure you control.

Our mission is to make AI feel like engineering, not magic. Systems that are observable, self-hostable, and honest about what they can and cannot do.
