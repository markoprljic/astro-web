02 · Knowledge Base & Company Knowledge Bank

Turn your company's scattered data into one searchable brain.

We vectorize and organize your company's data (files, Google Drive, Dropbox, Slack, WhatsApp, email and other correspondence, local databases and BI tools) into a single, unified knowledge base. Search it semantically or by keyword, plug it into your AI pipelines, or access it through an MCP server.

  • Chroma vector search
  • custom replicator/vectorizer engine
  • dedicated S3 buckets
  • management dashboard
  • semantic + text search
