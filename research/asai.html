Most businesses have no easy way to bring multi-agent AI assistants into their existing workflows without handing over control of their data and infrastructure. ASAI is a no-code assistant creation and management platform built to fix that: point-and-click setup, full vertical control over your data, software, hardware, and training data.

How it works

  1. Set up your assistant's personality
  2. Connect it to your platforms (Slack, Trello, Discord, Jira)
  3. Select and activate the tools and agents it will use
  4. Your assistant is ready to work

What sets it apart

Unlike LangChain, Auto-GPT, or Crew AI, ASAI combines a genuinely no-code interface with full self-hosting, multi-LLM support, extensive plugin integrations, and an open-source core, so non-technical teams get the same control that used to require a developer.
