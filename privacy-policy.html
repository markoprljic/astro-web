Who we are

Astro Synapse Limited, registered at 71 Lower Baggot Street, Dublin 2, Co. Dublin, D02 P593, Ireland ("Astro Synapse", "we", "us"), is the data controller for personal data collected through this website, and may act as a data processor for client data processed through our products and services (ASAI, Interpress, Gedor, webAgen, and custom integrations), depending on the engagement.

What we collect

  • Contact and enquiry data: name, email, company, and message content submitted through our contact form or waitlists.
  • Product usage data: for early access / waitlist products (ASAI, Gedor, Interpress), account and usage information needed to operate the service.
  • Client data processed on behalf of clients: documents, business data, and other content clients choose to connect to our AI systems (RAG pipelines, knowledge bases, translations) under a separate data processing agreement.

Why we collect it

  • To respond to enquiries and provide early access.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve our products.
  • To fulfil contractual obligations under client agreements, where we process data on a client's behalf.

Legal basis

  • Consent (waitlist sign-ups, marketing communications).
  • Contract performance (delivering a service you or your company requested).
  • Legitimate interest (operating and securing our website and products).
