ASAI

Early Access
001

Your personalized, multi-agent AI avatar.

Most businesses have no easy way to bring multi-agent AI assistants into their existing workflows without handing over control of their data and infrastructure. ASAI is a no-code assistant creation and management platform built to fix that: point-and-click setup, full vertical control over your data, software, hardware, and training data.

  • no-code
  • multi-agent
  • self-hosted
  • multi-LLM
  • open-source
Try ASAI now →

How it works

  1. Set up your assistant's personality
  2. Connect it to your platforms (Slack, Trello, Discord, Jira)
  3. Select and activate the tools and agents it will use
  4. Your assistant is ready to work

What sets it apart

Unlike LangChain, Auto-GPT, or Crew AI, ASAI combines a genuinely no-code interface with full self-hosting, multi-LLM support, extensive plugin integrations, and an open-source core, so non-technical teams get the same control that used to require a developer.

003

LLM as a Service. Hosted in the EU.

On-demand or private provisioning, a curated open-weight model catalog, and an OpenAI-compatible API. One key, one endpoint: switch models without touching your code.

  • OpenAI-compatible API
  • open-weight catalog
  • managed vector databases
  • GDPR & EU AI Act compliant
Join the waitlist →

Built for data sovereignty. Gedor runs entirely within the EU, subject only to EU legislation (GDPR, EU AI Act), with no foreign surveillance framework exposure, unlike US-based providers legally obligated to share data with US authorities on request.

Coming soon. A curated catalog of general purpose, coding, and embedding open-weight models, plus managed vector databases (Qdrant, Milvus, Weaviate, Chroma, and more) ready to pair with any model in the catalog.

What's next. AI Agents as a Service: ready-to-deploy agents for common workflows. Already have your own agent? Bring Your Own Agent and run it on EU infrastructure, fully compliant.
